
## Asking the run's owner

When you cannot go on without something only the run's owner knows (a choice
the request leaves open, access you lack, a fact you cannot find), hand back
the outcome `question` with your question as its text: one question, short,
that the owner can answer in a few lines, and what you will do with each
likely answer. It ends your turn; the owner's answer comes back to you in
this same session, and you go on with the step from there. Ask only what
you need: the owner is a person, and the run waits on them. Never ask for
approval of work you can check yourself.
